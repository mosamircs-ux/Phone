# Pulse Dial

<img alt="Pulse Dial Logo" src="graphics/pulse_dial_icon_512.png" width="120" />

**Smart. Fast. Modern Calling.**

Pulse Dial is a modern, dark-first Android Phone & Dialer app designed with a Deep Ocean Indigo & Electric Teal Material 3 aesthetic, fast T9 dialpad search, call screening, speed dial, dual-SIM support, and full call history management.

## Highlights

- **Deep Ocean Indigo & Electric Teal Design**: Elevated Material 3 cards, pill-shaped T9 dialpad, and dark-first visual polish.
- **Fast T9 Smart Dialpad**: Instant contact matching, speed dial shortcuts, haptic feedback, and DTMF tones.
- **Full In-Call Experience**: Mute, speaker/Bluetooth audio routing, hold, swap, merge, conference management, and swipe-to-answer.
- **Call Screening & Blocking**: Built-in call screening service and blocked numbers management.
- **Call History Export & Import**: Back up and restore call logs in JSON format.
